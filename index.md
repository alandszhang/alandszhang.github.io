## About Me {#about}

I am currently a Research Fellow at [Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/). Previously, I was an AI Researcher in Embodied Robotics at Sony.

Prior to Sony, I worked at the Hyundai-NTU-A*STAR Corporate Lab with [Prof. Kong Wai-Kin Adams](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/AdamsKong/), and led the SLAM research group at the [HESL, CCDS, NTU](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/hesl) under [Prof. Lam Siew Kei](https://siewkeilam.github.io/ei-research-group/index.html). My research focuses on AI for Robotics, with a particular interest in Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, Robot Localization and Perception, Visual Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM), and Visual Place Recognition (VPR).

I earned my Ph.D. from [CCDS](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/computing), [NTU, Singapore](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/) in Jan 2026, under the supervision of [Prof. Lam Siew Kei](https://siewkeilam.github.io/ei-research-group/index.html), and my M.S. in Electronic Information Engineering (EIE) from [City University of Hong Kong (CityUHK)](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/) in Oct 2019, under the supervision of [Prof. So Hing Cheung](https://www.ee.cityu.edu.hk/~hcso/) (IEEE Fellow).

Previously, I worked at [CamSense (欢创科技)](https://www.camsense.cn/en/) as an algorithm engineer from 2017 to 2018, and at [RoboMaster](https://www.robomaster.com/en-US), [DJI (大疆创新)](https://www.dji.com/) as an embedded software engineer from 2016 to 2017.

## Research Interests {#research}

- **Embodied AI:** Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and AI for robotics.
- **Robot Localization & Perception:** visual SLAM and visual place recognition (VPR).
